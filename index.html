About

I’m a software engineer with over ten years of professional experience building applications in Java. I studied Computer Science at the University of Zielona Góra and, while still a student, started working for leading ERP companies in Poland, which is where my programming skills took shape.

Since then I’ve worked with Java, AWS, Docker and the popular frameworks around them, building and leading the development of systems used by thousands of people across Poland and Europe. I care about high-quality code: easy to understand, easy to maintain, and open to change and further improvement.

SB Dev Works is where I build my own products. I make Android apps in Kotlin and Jetpack Compose, web apps in React and TypeScript, and the Kotlin and Ktor servers that sit behind them. Every project here is built the same careful way: a clean, layered architecture, automated tests for the rules that matter, and a CI pipeline with code-quality and security checks on every change.

I keep growing as both an engineer and a team leader: deepening what I already know and learning new technologies along the way.
