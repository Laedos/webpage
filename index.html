About

I’m an independent developer. I build Android apps in Kotlin and Jetpack Compose, web apps in React and TypeScript, and the Kotlin and Ktor servers that sit behind them.

Every project here is built the same careful way: a clean, layered architecture, automated tests for the rules that matter, and a CI pipeline with code-quality and security checks on every change. The apps work offline where that makes sense and stay simple to use.
