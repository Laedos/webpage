Odomo

Every vehicle’s maintenance, costs and paperwork in one Android app.

Android Kotlin Jetpack Compose Ktor

Screenshots

Overview

Odomo keeps track of what your cars cost and what they need. Log maintenance, repairs, fuel and expenses per vehicle, set budgets, record road trips, and get reminded before documents expire.

It works fully offline, with optional sync across devices for subscribers.

Key features

  • Vehicle profiles with maintenance, expense and planned-repair tracking
  • A fuel log with consumption and cost per distance
  • Budgets with progress tracking
  • Road trips with mileage and photos
  • Documents with issue and expiry dates, and reminders before they’re due
  • Statistics: spending breakdowns and per-vehicle summaries
  • Quick Add, for logging an expense, service or fill-up in seconds
  • Works offline, with zip backup and export, plus optional multi-device sync

Built with

Android app
Kotlin, Jetpack Compose, Material 3, Hilt, Room, DataStore
Sync server
Kotlin, Ktor, Exposed, PostgreSQL
Architecture
