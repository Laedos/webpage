Brown Map

A silly social map of every place you have ever pooped. Pin each throne, rate it, see where your friends went, and poop together with your group in a synchronised Squad Drop.

React Native Expo Kotlin Ktor

Screenshots

Overview

Brown Map is a joke app taken seriously. Every time nature calls, one tap drops an emoji pin where you are, rated from “never again” to “royal throne”. Over time your map fills up with every office, train, forest and grandma’s bathroom you have ever visited.

The fun is in sharing it: friends see each other’s pins, groups get their own map and feed, and a Squad Drop pings the whole group to go at once and keeps score. The jokes are loud, but location is treated with care: nothing is public, and pins near home are blurred before anyone else sees them.

Everything below is built and running on Android; the store releases come next.

Key features

  • One tap logs a drop where you are, with an emoji pin, a 1–5 throne rating, a place and a note
  • Your own map of every drop, plus a history to look back on, edit or quietly flush
  • Each drop is for friends, one of your groups, or just for you
  • Friends and their pins on the map, with your pins near home blurred to an area
  • Groups with their own map, a feed and reactions
  • Squad Drops: one tap pings the group, and whoever drops within 15 minutes counts as synchronised
  • Streaks, badges for countries and cities, and a group leaderboard
