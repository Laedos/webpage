Arcade

Small games that run in the browser: no installs, no accounts. Pick one from the menu and play.

TypeScript Canvas Vite Web

Play now

Overview

Arcade is a collection of quick browser games at play.sbdevworks.com: seven so far, each one learnable in seconds, and each with its own twist on a classic.

Key features

  • Slingwell: circle a planet and let go at the right moment to catch the next orbit; skip planets for bonus points
  • Stacker: drop the sliding block; the overhang is cut off, and perfect drops earn width back
  • Mirror Snake: classic snake, but every fourth apple flips your controls for six seconds
  • Lights Out: the 5x5 light-flipping puzzle, level after level, always solvable
  • 2048: slide and merge tiles to reach 2048
  • Brick Breaker: aim the ball off your paddle to clear every brick, level after level
  • Four in a Row: two players on one screen, taking turns to line up four
  • Online party games in preview: Doodle Telephone, Quiz Night, Imposter and Rock Paper Scissors, with every screen ready to click through while online rooms get set up
  • Best scores saved in your browser
  • Works on phone and desktop, with nothing to install and no account

Built with

TypeScript, Vite, the Canvas 2D API and plain DOM, with no framework and no backend. Game rules are kept separate from rendering and are unit-tested with Vitest. Hosted on GitHub Pages.
