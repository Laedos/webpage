Overview

Arcade is a growing collection of quick browser games at play.sbdevworks.com. Each one is learnable in seconds and puts its own twist on a classic, and they all work on a phone as well as a desktop.

Online party games are in preview: every screen is built and can be clicked through, while the room server gets ready to go live.

Key features

  • Slingwell: circle a planet and let go at the right moment to catch the next orbit
  • Stacker, Mirror Snake, 2048 and Brick Breaker: classics with a twist, each with a saved best score
  • Lights Out: the 5×5 light-flipping puzzle, level after level, always solvable
  • Four in a Row: two players on one screen
  • Online in preview: Doodle Telephone, Quiz Night, Imposter and Rock Paper Scissors, played with a room code
  • Keyboard controls on desktop, touch controls on phones
