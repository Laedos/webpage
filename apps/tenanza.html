Tenanza

One shared place for landlords and tenants: leases, rent, documents and maintenance.

Android Next.js Kotlin Ktor

Overview

Tenanza puts both sides of a rental on the same page. A landlord invites a tenant to a lease with a link, and from then on rent statements, documents, meter readings and maintenance requests live in one shared place instead of email threads.

It comes as an Android app and a web app, both on the same account.

Key features

  • Invite a tenant to a lease with a link; they don’t need an account first
  • Monthly rent statements with itemized charges, automatic reminders and late fees
  • A shared document vault for leases, IDs, receipts and property files
  • Maintenance requests from tenants, tracked through to done
  • Meter readings and move-in/move-out handover records, with PDF export
  • Leases in different currencies, with per-currency reports and tax exports
  • Web app in English, Polish, German and Ukrainian
