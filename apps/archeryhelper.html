ArcheryHelper

Personal archery equipment and session management for Android — bows, strings, arrows, accessories, maintenance, sessions, and scoring, all in one place.

Android Kotlin Jetpack Compose Material 3

Overview

ArcheryHelper is an offline-first Android app for archers to track their gear and practice. It covers equipment (bows, strings, arrows, accessories) with maintenance history, scored practice sessions, sight marks, tuning records, and a spine/ballistics calculator — with optional multi-device sync for subscribers.

Key features

  • Equipment tracking for bows, strings, arrows, and accessories with maintenance history
  • Session logging with per-end scoring and conditions (wind, temperature, indoor/outdoor)
  • Sight marks (distance → sight-setting lookup) and paper/bare-shaft/French tuning records
  • Arrow spine and ballistics calculator from draw weight, length, and point weight
  • Structured round formats — WA 720, Vegas 300, Indoor 18m — with personal-best tracking
  • Offline-first, with local zip backup/restore and optional multi-device sync

Built with

Kotlin, Jetpack Compose, Material 3, MVVM + Clean Architecture, Hilt, Room, DataStore, Navigation Compose.
