ArcheryHelper

Everything an archer keeps track of, in one Android app: gear, practice, scores and tuning.

Android Kotlin Jetpack Compose Ktor

Screenshots

Overview

ArcheryHelper keeps an archer’s equipment and practice in one place. Log bows, strings, arrows and accessories with their maintenance history, score practice sessions end by end, and keep sight marks and tuning notes to hand on the line.

It works fully offline, with optional sync across devices for subscribers.

Key features

  • Equipment tracking for bows, strings, arrows and accessories, with maintenance history
  • Practice sessions scored end by end, with conditions (wind, temperature, indoor or outdoor)
  • Standard rounds (WA 720, Vegas 300, Indoor 18 m) with personal-best tracking
  • Sight marks: a distance-to-setting lookup for the field
  • Paper, bare-shaft and French tuning records
  • An arrow spine and ballistics calculator from draw weight, arrow length and point weight
  • Works offline, with zip backup and restore, plus optional multi-device sync
