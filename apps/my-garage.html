My Garage

Personal garage management for Android — vehicles, maintenance, expenses, repairs, budgets, road trips, documents, and fuel tracking.

Android Kotlin Jetpack Compose Material 3

Overview

My Garage keeps every vehicle's maintenance, expenses, repairs, and paperwork in one offline-first app. It shares its architecture and engineering conventions with ArcheryHelper, and adds budgets, road trips, and fuel logging on top of the same equipment-tracking foundation.

Key features

  • Vehicle profiles with maintenance, expense, and planned-repair tracking
  • Budgets with progress tracking, and road trips with mileage and photos
  • Documents with issue/expiry dates and reminders for what's due
  • Statistics: spending breakdown and per-vehicle summaries
  • Quick Add bottom sheet for fast logging of expenses, maintenance, or fuel
  • Fuel log with consumption and cost-per-distance tracking
  • Offline-first, with full backup/export to a zip file

Built with

Kotlin, Jetpack Compose, Material 3, MVVM + Clean Architecture, Hilt, Room, DataStore, Navigation Compose.
