Dzieńki

A preschool’s day in parents’ pockets: meals, naps, messages, absences and who is picking up, for Polish preschools and the families they serve.

React Native Expo Kotlin Ktor

Screenshots

Overview

Dzieńki (Polish for “little days”) connects a preschool with the families it looks after. Teachers record the day as it happens — who arrived, what they ate, how long they slept — and parents see it on their phones the same afternoon, along with messages from the group’s teachers and announcements from the director.

It is built around how a preschool group actually works: one shared tablet per group with a personal PIN for each teacher, entries made for the whole group in one tap, and the day-to-day logistics of absences, meals and pickups handled in the same place. Child data is treated with care: health details only with a parent’s consent, nothing personal in notifications, and every change logged.

Key features

  • A daily report for every child: arrival and departure, meals, naps, mood, activities and notes
  • Whole-group entry for teachers, then just the exceptions — on a shared group tablet with personal PINs
  • Private conversations between each family and the group’s teachers, plus preschool and group announcements
  • Absence reporting that cancels meals when it arrives before the morning cut-off, with the kitchen’s daily count
  • Who is collecting the child today, with ID checks and court pickup restrictions enforced at the door
  • A shared calendar and the weekly menu, with each child’s allergies right next to it
  • Invite codes for staff and parents, an audit log, data export and account deletion
