PollDrop

Create a poll in seconds and share the link. No account needed.

React TypeScript Kotlin Ktor

Overview

PollDrop is for quick decisions: make an anonymous poll, share the link, and watch the results come in.

Registered creators get more control: private polls with invites, drafts, scheduled closing, and a choice of when voters get to see the results.

Key features

  • Anonymous polls in seconds, with no account (they expire after 90 days)
  • Templates for common polls
  • Private polls, shared by invite link or email
  • Drafts, scheduled closing, and archive
  • Results shown live, after voting, after closing, or kept hidden
  • Live results with CSV export
  • Report button for abusive public polls; repeatedly reported polls are hidden until reviewed

Built with

Web app
React, TypeScript, Vite, TanStack Query, Tailwind CSS
Server
Kotlin, Ktor, Exposed, Flyway, PostgreSQL
