PollDrop

A lightweight web app for creating and sharing polls — instant and anonymous, or managed by a registered creator.

Kotlin Ktor React TypeScript PostgreSQL

Overview

PollDrop lets anyone spin up an anonymous poll in seconds with no account needed, while registered creators get private polls with invites, full lifecycle management, and configurable results visibility.

Key features

  • Anonymous polls — instant creation, no account, 90-day expiry
  • Poll templates for quick starts
  • Registered creators: private polls with invite links or email, draft/open/close/archive lifecycle
  • Scheduled auto-close and configurable results visibility (hidden, after vote, after close, or live)
  • Live results with CSV export

Built with

Backend: Kotlin, Ktor, Exposed, Flyway, PostgreSQL. Frontend: React, TypeScript, Vite, TanStack Query, Tailwind CSS.
