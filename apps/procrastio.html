procrast.io

A focus timer for your desktop that also blocks distracting websites, with an Android companion app.

Kotlin JavaFX Android Ktor

Overview

procrast.io helps you stay on task at your computer. Start a focus session, and the sites that eat your time stay blocked until the break.

It sits quietly in the system tray, and a companion Android app keeps your tasks and notes in sync across devices.

Key features

  • Focus sessions with work and break phases
  • Website blocking: paste a link, and the site’s www and mobile addresses are blocked with it
  • A one-time setup, so blocking doesn’t ask for admin rights every session
  • Lives in the system tray: closing the window minimizes it instead of quitting
  • Tasks, Kanban cards and inbox notes, synced between desktop and Android
  • Free to use, with a Pro tier for multi-device sync

Built with

Desktop app
Kotlin, JavaFX, AtlantaFX, Ikonli
Android app
Kotlin, Jetpack Compose
Sync server
